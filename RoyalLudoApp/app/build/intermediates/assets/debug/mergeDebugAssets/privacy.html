Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Royal Ludo Club ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our services:

  • Account Credentials: If you sign in via email or Google auth, we store your profile details (name, avatar choice, stats).
  • Gameplay Data: Match records, coin counts, level progress, unlocked achievements, and power configurations.
  • Device Information: We may detect device parameters (screen size, OS, network latency) to ensure optimal WebView rendering and lobby re-connection.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Maintain your profile stats, ranking (ELO), and shop inventory via Firebase Firestore.
  • Synchronize online game boards and match states in real time using Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Perform client-side anti-cheat checks and host-side game room validations.
  • Provide service recovery during network reconnects or device battery events.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or lease your personal information. Your game name and stats are shared globally on our in-app public leaderboards. Real-time game configurations are shared only with the players in your active multiplayer lobby.

4. Data Deletion

You can choose to delete your account or guest profile history by contacting us or clearing your local application storage. Signing out resets guest progression stored in local storage cache.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest update date will be modified at the top of this document.
